A note from the founders Page 02

A note from the founders

Most agencies sell activity. We sell customers.

We started Brandify after seeing the same pattern at too many businesses: a good product, a decent website, and a monthly report full of impressions that never turned into revenue.

So we built the agency we wished those businesses had. Strategy, creative and tracking sit in the same room. When a campaign underperforms, we change the plan instead of changing the slide deck.

Today we work with startups, local businesses and established brands. The size varies, but the rule stays the same: if we can't show how the work moved your numbers, it doesn't count.

Four rules we work by Page 03

The rules behind the growth

  1. 01 /

    Revenue is the scoreboard

    Traffic, followers and impressions only matter when they lead to enquiries and sales. We set goals around leads, cost per customer and return on ad spend before we launch anything.

  2. 02 /

    Plain-English reporting

    Live dashboards and a monthly report that says what worked, what didn't and what we're changing next.

  3. 03 /

    No lock-in

    Flexible agreements. You stay because the results are good, not because of a contract.

  4. 04 /

    One team, every channel

    Your SEO lead, ad manager and designer share a plan, so the ad, the landing page and the follow-up all say the same thing.
